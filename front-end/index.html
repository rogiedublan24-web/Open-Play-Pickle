What to bring

  • Your paddle (if you have one)
  • Rubber or court shoes
  • Water to drink
  • A towel and a change of clothes
  • A good attitude

Court etiquette

  • Arrive early, before your game starts
  • Respect all players
  • Follow the schedule and the organizer's instructions
  • Clean up your own gear and trash
  • If you can't make it, let us know so someone else can take your slot
